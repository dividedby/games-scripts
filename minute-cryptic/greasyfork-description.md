Two things for **[Minute Cryptic](https://www.minutecryptic.com)** in the browser, mostly
for playing on a phone. It never looks at the answer and gives no hints: it only moves the
letters you've typed.

**Your phone's own keyboard**

- The game's on-screen keyboard is hidden; tap an answer box and your phone's keyboard
  comes up instead. **Done** closes it.
- A **⌨** button next to **check** switches back to the game's keyboard.

**A quicker scribble space**

- **Shuffle** also shuffles the letters in the answer boxes, in place.
- **Circle** takes every letter out of the boxes and lays them out in a shuffled circle, in
  one tap.
- **⤓** (next to Shuffle) puts the loose letters back in the empty boxes.
- Letters revealed by a hint always stay put.
